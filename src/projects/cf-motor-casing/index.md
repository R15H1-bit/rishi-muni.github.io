## Why composite

{# Mass savings vs. aluminum, burst margin, what motor it's for. #}

A carbon-fiber case can cut inert mass substantially compared to metal, but it moves the hard questions to the laminate and the bonds — especially the carbon–epoxy interfaces that see heat during and after the burn.
